Auli and Joshimath

Snow and views near Joshimath. Check subsidence advisories.

Check before you go: driving times are typical estimates for mountain roads, not live traffic. Confirm road status, opening dates and bookings locally.

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Rishikesh to Joshimath. A long drive via Devprayag.
    Driving: About 9-10 hours (250 km) · Overnight: Joshimath or Pipalkoti
  2. Day 2: Auli. Explore Auli and Gorson Bugyal.
    Driving: About 1 hour · Overnight: Auli
  3. Day 3: Auli and optional Badrinath day trip. Optional visit to Badrinath if open.
    Driving: About 2-3 hours each way · Overnight: Auli or Joshimath
  4. Day 4: Return. Drive back to Rishikesh.
    Driving: About 9-10 hours · Overnight: Rishikesh

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Balanced. Balanced; two long driving days.
