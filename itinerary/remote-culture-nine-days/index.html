Day by day

  1. Day 1: Kathgodam to Almora. Drive and bazaar.
    Driving: About 3-3.5 hours · Overnight: Almora
  2. Day 2: Almora and Jageshwar. Temples.
    Driving: About 1 hour each way · Overnight: Almora
  3. Day 3: Almora to Pithoragarh. Long drive.
    Driving: About 5-6 hours · Overnight: Pithoragarh
  4. Day 4: Pithoragarh to Chaukori. Quiet stop.
    Driving: About 3-4 hours · Overnight: Chaukori
  5. Day 5: Chaukori to Munsiyari. Mountain road.
    Driving: About 5-6 hours · Overnight: Munsiyari
  6. Day 6: Munsiyari. Khaliya Top walk.
    Driving: Walk · Overnight: Munsiyari
  7. Day 7: Munsiyari. Heritage museum and village walk.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Munsiyari
  8. Day 8: Munsiyari to Almora. Long drive back.
    Driving: About 8-9 hours · Overnight: Almora
  9. Day 9: Return. Back to Kathgodam.
    Driving: About 3-3.5 hours · Overnight: None

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Balanced. Balanced; long drives on days 3, 5 and 8.

Monsoon landslides are common on this route.
