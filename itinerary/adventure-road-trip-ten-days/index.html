Day by day

  1. Day 1-2: Rishikesh. Rafting and camping.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Rishikesh
  2. Day 3: Rishikesh to Chopta. Long drive.
    Driving: About 8-9 hours · Overnight: Chopta
  3. Day 4: Tungnath and Chandrashila. Trek.
    Driving: Walk · Overnight: Chopta
  4. Day 5: Chopta to Joshimath. Mountain drive.
    Driving: About 5-6 hours · Overnight: Joshimath
  5. Day 6: Auli. Meadow walk and views.
    Driving: About 1 hour · Overnight: Auli
  6. Day 7-9: Valley of Flowers trek (seasonal). Three-day trek from Govindghat.
    Driving: About 1 hour then walk · Overnight: Ghangaria
  7. Day 10: Return. Long drive to Rishikesh.
    Driving: About 9-10 hours · Overnight: None

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Rushed. Rushed: several long drives; add buffer days.

Seasonal alternatives

The Valley of Flowers is open only June-October; replace with Auli extra days outside that.
