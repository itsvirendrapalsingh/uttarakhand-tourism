Day by day

  1. Day 1: Ghats, bridges and aarti. Morning at Laxman Jhula and Ram Jhula, lunch at a cafe in Tapovan, then the Beatles Ashram if open. Finish with the Ganga aarti at Triveni Ghat.
    Driving: Local; short rides · Overnight: None (day trip)

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Comfortable. Comfortable for one day. Add a second day for rafting or yoga.

Seasonal alternatives

In monsoon the river is high; skip river activities.
