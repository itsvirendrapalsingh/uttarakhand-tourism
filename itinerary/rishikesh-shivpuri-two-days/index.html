Day by day

  1. Day 1: Rishikesh town. Ghats, bridges and the evening aarti.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Rishikesh
  2. Day 2: Shivpuri rafting. Book a rafting trip from Shivpuri side with a licensed operator, and finish at Rishikesh.
    Driving: About 30-45 minutes by road · Overnight: Return or camp by the river

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Comfortable. Comfortable; confirm operator licence and safety gear.

Seasonal alternatives

Rafting is generally suspended in monsoon.
