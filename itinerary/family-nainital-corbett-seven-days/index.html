Family holiday: Nainital and Corbett

Wildlife, lakes and a quiet hill station for families.

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Delhi to Corbett. Drive to Ramnagar.
    Driving: About 5-6 hours · Overnight: Corbett
  2. Day 2: Safari and rest. Morning safari; relax by the river.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Corbett
  3. Day 3: Corbett to Nainital. Drive up and boating.
    Driving: About 2.5-3 hours · Overnight: Nainital
  4. Day 4: Nainital. Sights and shopping.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Nainital
  5. Day 5: Bhimtal. Lake and aquarium.
    Driving: About 45 minutes · Overnight: Bhimtal
  6. Day 6: Ranikhet. Gardens and golf.
    Driving: About 2.5 hours · Overnight: Ranikhet
  7. Day 7: Return. Back to Delhi by road or via Kathgodam.
    Driving: About 7-8 hours · Overnight: None

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Comfortable. Comfortable; kid-friendly pacing.

Seasonal alternatives

Most Corbett core zones are closed in the monsoon (only Jhirna, Dhela and Garjia stay open all year); check zone dates on corbettgov.org.
