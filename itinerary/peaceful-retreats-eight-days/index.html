Day by day

  1. Day 1: Dehradun to Kanatal. Drive and settle in.
    Driving: About 2.5-3 hours · Overnight: Kanatal
  2. Day 2: Kanatal. Forest walk and Surkanda Devi.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Kanatal
  3. Day 3: Kanatal to Dhanaulti. Short drive.
    Driving: About 1 hour · Overnight: Dhanaulti
  4. Day 4: Dhanaulti. Eco Parks and rest.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Dhanaulti
  5. Day 5: Dhanaulti to Chakrata. Drive through forest.
    Driving: About 3-4 hours · Overnight: Chakrata
  6. Day 6: Chakrata. Tiger Falls and Deoban.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Chakrata
  7. Day 7: Chakrata to Dehradun. Return drive.
    Driving: About 3-4 hours · Overnight: Dehradun
  8. Day 8: Departure. Depart.
    Driving: None · Overnight: None
