Nainital sightseeing in one day

Boating, a viewpoint and Mall Road.

Home / Itineraries / Nainital sightseeing in one day

Check before you go: driving times are typical estimates for mountain roads, not live traffic. Confirm road status, opening dates and bookings locally.

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Lake, temple and viewpoints. Boat on Naini Lake in the morning, visit Naina Devi, take the cable car or walk to Snow View Point, and finish on Mall Road.
    Driving: Local; mostly walkable · Overnight: None (day trip)

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Comfortable. Comfortable if you are already in Nainital.

Seasonal alternatives

Rain can close the cable car; have an indoor plan.
