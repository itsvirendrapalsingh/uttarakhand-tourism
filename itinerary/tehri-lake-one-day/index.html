Day by day

  1. Day 1: Rishikesh to Tehri and back. Leave early, reach the lake in the late morning, do one activity, then start back. Keep an hour of buffer for mountain roads.
    Driving: About 2.5-3 hours each way (75-80 km) · Overnight: None (day trip)

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Rushed. Rushed; make it two days if you can.

Seasonal alternatives

Avoid heavy monsoon days because of landslides.
