Chopta, Tungnath and Chandrashila

An easy trek and a forest retreat.

Home / Itineraries / Chopta, Tungnath and Chandrashila

Check before you go: driving times are typical estimates for mountain roads, not live traffic. Confirm road status, opening dates and bookings locally.

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Rishikesh to Chopta. A long drive via Rudraprayag and Ukhimath.
    Driving: About 8-9 hours (220 km) · Overnight: Chopta
  2. Day 2: Tungnath and Chandrashila. Early walk to Tungnath and on to Chandrashila.
    Driving: Walk · Overnight: Chopta
  3. Day 3: Return. Drive back.
    Driving: About 8-9 hours · Overnight: Rishikesh

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Balanced. Balanced; two long driving days.

Seasonal alternatives

In winter the temple is closed and the trail is snowy.
