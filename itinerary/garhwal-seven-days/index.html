Day by day

  1. Day 1: Dehradun. Arrive and rest.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Dehradun
  2. Day 2: Mussoorie. Sights and Mall Road.
    Driving: About 1.5-2 hours · Overnight: Mussoorie
  3. Day 3: Mussoorie to Kanatal or Dhanaulti. Quiet forest stay.
    Driving: About 1.5-2 hours · Overnight: Kanatal
  4. Day 4: Tehri Lake. Lake and activities.
    Driving: About 2-2.5 hours · Overnight: New Tehri
  5. Day 5: Rishikesh. Ghats and aarti.
    Driving: About 2.5-3 hours · Overnight: Rishikesh
  6. Day 6: Rishikesh to Haridwar. Temples and Har Ki Pauri.
    Driving: About 1 hour · Overnight: Haridwar
  7. Day 7: Departure. Return to Delhi.
    Driving: About 4-5 hours · Overnight: None
