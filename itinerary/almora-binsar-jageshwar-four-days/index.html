Almora, Binsar and Jageshwar

Temples, sweets and forest in Kumaon.

Home / Itineraries / Almora, Binsar and Jageshwar

Check before you go: driving times are typical estimates for mountain roads, not live traffic. Confirm road status, opening dates and bookings locally.

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Kathgodam to Almora. Drive up; evening in the bazaar for bal mithai.
    Driving: About 3-3.5 hours · Overnight: Almora
  2. Day 2: Kasar Devi and Almora. Morning at Kasar Devi and Chitai temple.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Almora
  3. Day 3: Binsar. Zero Point and forest walks.
    Driving: About 1-1.5 hours · Overnight: Binsar
  4. Day 4: Jageshwar and return. Visit Jageshwar, then return.
    Driving: About 4-5 hours back to Kathgodam · Overnight: Return

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Balanced. Balanced; the last day has long driving.

Seasonal alternatives

Winters are cold; Binsar can be snowy.
