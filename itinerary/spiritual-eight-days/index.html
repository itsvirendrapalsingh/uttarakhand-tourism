Day by day

  1. Day 1: Haridwar. Evening aarti at Har Ki Pauri.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Haridwar
  2. Day 2: Haridwar to Rishikesh. Temples and ashrams.
    Driving: About 1 hour · Overnight: Rishikesh
  3. Day 3: Rishikesh. Yoga class and aarti.
    Driving: Local · Overnight: Rishikesh
  4. Day 4: Devprayag and Rudraprayag. Confluences.
    Driving: About 4-5 hours · Overnight: Rudraprayag or Ukhimath
  5. Day 5: Chopta. Drive and rest.
    Driving: About 3 hours · Overnight: Chopta
  6. Day 6: Tungnath. Trek to the temple.
    Driving: Walk · Overnight: Chopta
  7. Day 7: Return to Rishikesh. Long drive.
    Driving: About 7-8 hours · Overnight: Rishikesh
  8. Day 8: Departure. Haridwar or onward.
    Driving: About 1 hour · Overnight: None

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Comfortable. Relaxed except for Day 7.

Seasonal alternatives

In winter the Tungnath temple is closed.
