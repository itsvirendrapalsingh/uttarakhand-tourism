Char Dham Yatra in 11 days

The traditional circuit by road with a walk to Yamunotri and Kedarnath. Registration is required.

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Haridwar to Barkot. Long drive via Dehradun and Mussoorie.
    Driving: About 8-9 hours · Overnight: Barkot
  2. Day 2: Yamunotri. Drive to Janki Chatti and walk to the temple; return to Barkot.
    Driving: About 1.5-2 hours each way plus a 5-6 km walk each way · Overnight: Barkot
  3. Day 3: Barkot to Uttarkashi. Moderate driving day.
    Driving: About 4-5 hours · Overnight: Uttarkashi
  4. Day 4: Gangotri. Visit the temple and return.
    Driving: About 3-4 hours each way · Overnight: Uttarkashi or Harsil
  5. Day 5: Uttarkashi to Guptkashi. Long drive via Tehri.
    Driving: About 8-9 hours · Overnight: Guptkashi
  6. Day 6: Guptkashi to Sonprayag, walk to Kedarnath. Walk or helicopter option; overnight at Kedarnath.
    Driving: About 1.5 hours to Sonprayag, then 16 km walk · Overnight: Kedarnath
  7. Day 7: Kedarnath darshan and descend. Descend after darshan.
    Driving: Walk plus 1.5 hours · Overnight: Guptkashi
  8. Day 8: Guptkashi to Joshimath. Long drive via Chamoli.
    Driving: About 6-7 hours · Overnight: Joshimath or Pipalkoti
  9. Day 9: Badrinath. Darshan, Mana and return.
    Driving: About 1.5-2 hours each way · Overnight: Joshimath
  10. Day 10: Joshimath to Rishikesh. Long drive back.
    Driving: About 9-10 hours · Overnight: Rishikesh
  11. Day 11: Departure. Return to Haridwar or onward.
    Driving: About 1 hour · Overnight: None

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Balanced. Balanced on paper but weather can force extra days. Add 1-2 buffer days.

Seasonal alternatives

Open only April/May to October/November; monsoon has landslide risk. Add buffer days.
