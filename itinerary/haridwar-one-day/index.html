Day by day

  1. Day 1: Mansa Devi, Chandi Devi and Har Ki Pauri. Take a ropeway up to Mansa Devi in the morning, visit Chandi Devi or Maya Devi, then reach Har Ki Pauri early for the dusk aarti.
    Driving: Local; short rides · Overnight: None (day trip)

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Comfortable. Comfortable. Expect heavy crowds on festival days.
