Day by day

  1. Day 1: Rishikesh to Joshimath. Long drive.
    Driving: About 9-10 hours · Overnight: Joshimath
  2. Day 2: Govindghat to Ghangaria. 13 km walk.
    Driving: About 1 hour plus walk · Overnight: Ghangaria
  3. Day 3: Valley of Flowers. Walk in and return.
    Driving: Walk · Overnight: Ghangaria
  4. Day 4: Hemkund Sahib. Steep climb and return.
    Driving: Walk · Overnight: Ghangaria
  5. Day 5: Ghangaria to Joshimath. Walk down and drive.
    Driving: Walk plus 1 hour · Overnight: Joshimath
  6. Day 6: Joshimath to Rishikesh. Long drive.
    Driving: About 9-10 hours · Overnight: Rishikesh
  7. Day 7: Buffer. Buffer day for landslides or rest.
    Driving: None · Overnight: None

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Balanced. Balanced; the buffer day is important.
