Mussoorie and Dhanaulti

Mussoorie for the town and Dhanaulti for the forest.

Home / Itineraries / Mussoorie and Dhanaulti

Check before you go: driving times are typical estimates for mountain roads, not live traffic. Confirm road status, opening dates and bookings locally.

Day by day

  1. Day 1: Dehradun to Mussoorie. Drive up, visit Gun Hill and Camel's Back Road, evening on Mall Road.
    Driving: About 1.5-2 hours (35 km) · Overnight: Mussoorie
  2. Day 2: Mussoorie to Dhanaulti and return. Drive to Dhanaulti, visit Eco Parks and Surkanda Devi if time, then return to Dehradun.
    Driving: About 1-1.5 hours to Dhanaulti and 2-3 hours back to Dehradun · Overnight: Return, or stay at Dhanaulti

Is it comfortable or rushed?

Balanced. Balanced; the second day has more driving.

Seasonal alternatives

In winter Dhanaulti is likelier to have snow; check road conditions.
