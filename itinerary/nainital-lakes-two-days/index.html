Day by day

  1. Day 1: Kathgodam to Nainital. Drive up, boating on Naini Lake, evening on Mall Road.
    Driving: About 1-1.5 hours (35 km) · Overnight: Nainital
  2. Day 2: Bhimtal and Sattal. Visit Bhimtal, then Sattal for a forest walk, and head back to Kathgodam.
    Driving: About 1-1.5 hours between places · Overnight: Return or stay at Sattal
