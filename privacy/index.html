Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and the choices you have

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Uttarakhand Tourism Center (uttarakhandtourismcenter.com, "we", "us") is an independent travel guide to Uttarakhand. We do not run user accounts or a customer database. This policy explains the limited information that is processed when you use the site.

Information we collect

  • Usage data (Google Analytics). We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, time on page, approximate location (city or country), device and browser type, the site or search that sent you here, and clicks on our email, phone or WhatsApp links and contact-form submissions. We do not send your name, email address, phone number or message text to Google Analytics.
  • Messages you send us. The contact form does not upload anything to our servers. It opens your own email app with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address, name and whatever you write.
  • Saved pages. The Save button stores your list of saved pages in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting, which processes technical data such as IP address, browser type and the page requested in order to deliver the site and protect it against abuse.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_) to tell visits apart and measure returning visitors. If advertising is shown on the site, Google and its partners may also set advertising cookies (see the next section). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site still works without them. You can also opt out of Google Analytics on all websites with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

  • We use, or may use, Google AdSense to show advertisements on this site.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Third-party services that load in your browser

To display the site, your browser downloads some files directly from other providers, which receive your IP address and browser details when it does: Google Fonts (text fonts), Cloudflare cdnjs (icons and the map library), OpenStreetMap (map tiles on the map page) and Google Tag Manager (the Google Analytics script). Their own privacy policies apply.

How we use information

  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To see which pages are useful, fix problems and improve the guides.
  • To keep the site secure and working.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the site free.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google and the content providers listed), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. This includes your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and similar laws where you live. Email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is a general travel guide and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to official and third-party websites (for example, government registration portals). We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
