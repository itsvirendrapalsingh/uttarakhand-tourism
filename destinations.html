Destinations

Guides to towns, lakes, hill stations, shrines, parks and villages across Garhwal and Kumaon

Yamunotri

Source shrine of the River Yamuna, first stop of the traditional Char Dham yatra

Garhwal · about 3,293 m

Gangotri

Temple of Goddess Ganga and gateway to the Gaumukh glacier

Garhwal · about 3,100 m

Kedarnath

Shiva shrine in the Mandakini valley, reached by a long uphill walk or by helicopter

Garhwal · about 3,583 m (sources vary by a few metres)

Badrinath

Vishnu shrine on the Alaknanda, reachable by road

Garhwal · about 3,100 m

Dehradun

State capital and the main gateway to Mussoorie and the Garhwal hills

Garhwal · about 640 m

Mussoorie

Queen of the Hills, a colonial-era hill station above the Doon valley

Garhwal · about 2,000 m

Rishikesh

Yoga capital on the Ganga and gateway to the Char Dham routes

Garhwal · about 350 m

Haridwar

One of Hinduism's seven holiest cities, where the Ganga enters the plains

Garhwal · about 300 m

New Tehri and Tehri Lake

A planned town above the Tehri reservoir, one of India's tallest dams

Garhwal · about 1,750 m

Kanatal

Quiet village with orchards, camps and views on the Mussoorie-Chamba road

Garhwal · about 2,500 m

Dhanaulti

Deodar and pine forests above Mussoorie, quieter and cooler

Garhwal · about 2,200-2,300 m

Chakrata

Cantonment hill town with forest, waterfalls and relatively few visitors

Garhwal · about 2,100 m

Uttarkashi

Bhagirathi valley town, gateway to Gangotri and several treks

Garhwal · about 1,150 m

Harsil and Dharali

High Bhagirathi valley villages with apple orchards and Himalayan views

Garhwal · about 2,600 m

Joshimath

Winter seat of Badrinath and the base for Auli and the Valley of Flowers

Garhwal · about 1,900 m

Auli

Himalayan ski slope with views of Nanda Devi and neighbouring peaks

Garhwal · about 2,500-3,000 m

Chopta and Tungnath

Forest meadows and the highest Shiva temple, with an easy short trek

Garhwal · Chopta about 2,700 m; Tungnath about 3,680 m

Devprayag

The confluence where the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi form the Ganga

Garhwal · about 475 m

Khirsu and Pauri

Quiet village above Pauri with orchards, forest and Himalayan views

Garhwal · about 1,700 m

Lansdowne

Quiet cantonment hill station with a colonial feel

Garhwal · about 1,700 m

Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Sahib

UNESCO World Heritage alpine valley, and the Sikh shrine of Hemkund Sahib

Garhwal · Valley about 3,600 m; Hemkund Sahib about 4,300 m

Mana Village

The last motorable village before the Tibet border area, a short drive from Badrinath

Garhwal · about 3,200 m

Rajaji National Park

Forest reserve near Haridwar and Rishikesh, known for elephants

Garhwal · about 300-1,000 m

Nainital

Lake town around the pear-shaped Naini Lake

Kumaon · about 2,000 m

Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal

Larger, calmer lakes near Nainital

Kumaon · about 1,400 m

Sattal

A cluster of connected lakes in an oak forest, popular with birdwatchers

Kumaon · about 1,300 m

Mukteshwar

High ridge village with orchards and wide Himalayan views

Kumaon · about 2,300 m

Almora

Cultural town of Kumaon, known for sweets, temples and Himalayan views

Kumaon · about 1,650 m

Ranikhet

Calm cantonment hill station with pine forests and Himalayan views

Kumaon · about 1,800 m

Kausani

Ridge village with wide views of the Himalayan peaks

Kumaon · about 1,900 m

Binsar

Wildlife sanctuary and ridge with oak and rhododendron forest

Kumaon · about 2,400 m

Jageshwar

A forest valley with a group of old stone temples

Kumaon · about 1,870 m

Pithoragarh

Eastern Kumaon town in a wide valley, gateway to Munsiyari and the Kailash Mansarovar route

Kumaon · about 1,650 m

Munsiyari

Remote hill town facing the Panchachuli peaks, base for Johar valley treks

Kumaon · about 2,200 m

Chaukori

Quiet village with Himalayan views on the way to Munsiyari

Kumaon · about 2,000 m

Pangot and Kilbury

Birdwatching village a short drive from Nainital

Kumaon · about 1,950 m

Jim Corbett National Park

India's oldest national park, famous for tigers and elephants

Kumaon · about 400-1,200 m
