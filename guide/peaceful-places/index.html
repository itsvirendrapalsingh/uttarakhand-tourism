Peaceful, less-crowded places

Quieter villages and hill stations. Relatively quiet, never guaranteed empty

Home / Plan / Peaceful, less-crowded places

These places are generally quieter than Mussoorie or Nainital, especially on weekdays and in the off-season. Crowds vary by weekend, holiday and year, and we cannot guarantee any place will be quiet.

Khirsu (Pauri)

  • Why quiet: small village with orchards and few hotels.
  • Best months: March-June and September-November.
  • Stay: homestays and small resorts.
  • Reach: about 15 km from Pauri; Pauri is 4-5 hours from Rishikesh.
  • Network: patchy; confirm with host.
  • Suits: couples, families, remote workers with a backup connection.

Kanatal and Dhanaulti

  • Why quiet: small villages in forest, away from main towns.
  • Best months: March-June, September-November; snow chance in winter.
  • Stay: resorts, camps and homestays.
  • Reach: 75-80 km from Dehradun to Kanatal.
  • Suits: families and couples.

Chakrata

  • Why quiet: cantonment area with limited hotels.
  • Best months: March-June, September-November.
  • Reach: 90-100 km from Dehradun.
  • Note: some areas are restricted; carry ID.

Peora

  • Why quiet: small village near Nainital with orchards and walks.
  • Reach: about 40-50 km from Nainital by road.
  • Stay: homestays; limited options.
  • Network: patchy in places.

Pangot and Sitla

  • Why quiet: birdwatching villages close to Nainital.
  • Best months: October-April for birds.
  • Reach: Pangot is about 15 km from Nainital.

Chaukori and Munsiyari

  • Why quiet: far from main routes with long road journeys.
  • Best months: March-June, September-November.
  • Reach: very long drive from Kathgodam; plan overnight stops.
  • Network and ATMs: limited.

Binsar

  • Why quiet: forest sanctuary, few hotels.
  • Entry fee and rules apply.

Sari and Deoria Tal

  • Why quiet: small village near Chopta, base for Deoria Tal.
  • Best months: March-June, September-November.
  • Stay: homestays with local food.

Mana and Harsil

  • Mana: border village near Badrinath, a short visit with local culture.
  • Harsil: Bhagirathi valley village with orchards. Dharali and Harsil were hit by a flash flood on 5 August 2025; check lodging and road status.

Responsible tourism

  • Stay in locally owned homestays.
  • Respect local customs and sacred sites.
  • Carry out your rubbish; many villages have no waste collection.
  • Use water and electricity sparingly in remote places.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really quiet?

Quieter than the big hill stations, but not guaranteed. Visit on weekdays to avoid crowds.
