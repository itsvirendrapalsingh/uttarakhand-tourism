First-time visitor's guide to Uttarakhand

Planning, budget, packing, health and common mistakes

Home / Plan / First-time visitor's guide to Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand is a mountain state: distances look short on a map but take long on the road. This guide helps you plan realistically.

How to plan from scratch

  • Pick a month first: it decides which places are open and safe (see the best-time guide).
  • Choose one region (Garhwal or Kumaon) for a short trip; do not try to cover both in a week.
  • Limit driving to about 5-6 hours on mountain days.
  • Add one buffer day to any trip of five days or more.
  • Book accommodation first in peak months (May-June, October-November).

Choosing a destination by days

  • 1 day: Rishikesh, Haridwar, Mussoorie or Nainital.
  • 2-3 days: Mussoorie and Dhanaulti, Nainital and lakes, Rishikesh and Haridwar.
  • 4-6 days: Kumaon circuit, Auli, Chopta.
  • 7+ days: Garhwal or Kumaon loops, Char Dham, a trek.

Budget planning

Use the budget estimator on the Plan page with your own quoted prices. The main costs are vehicle hire, accommodation, meals and activities. Prices vary widely by season and place; get quotes and check them on official or booking sites.

Packing checklist

  • Layers: base, mid and a windproof outer layer
  • Rain jacket or poncho
  • Comfortable walking shoes with grip
  • Warm cap and gloves if above 2,000 m
  • Sun protection
  • Personal medication and a small first-aid kit
  • Power bank and offline maps
  • ID and copies of bookings

Altitude awareness

Altitude sickness can affect anyone above about 2,500 m. Ascend gradually, drink water, and rest. Descend if symptoms worsen. Consult a doctor first if you have heart, lung or blood-pressure conditions.

Money, network and emergencies

  • ATMs are common in towns but scarce in remote areas; carry cash.
  • Mobile coverage is patchy in valleys and above villages; download offline maps.
  • Emergency numbers: 112 (national emergency), 108 (ambulance). Confirm the current local helplines on official sites.
  • Consider travel insurance, especially for treks and monsoon travel.

Travelling with children and elders

  • Choose road-accessible destinations.
  • Shorter driving days and slower itineraries.
  • Check medical facilities along the route.

Common mistakes

  • Packing too many stops into too few days.
  • Driving at night.
  • Ignoring weather and landslide alerts.
  • Assuming ATMs, fuel and network are available everywhere.
  • Not booking in peak season.

Useful official resources

  • Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board
  • India Meteorological Department (weather and rainfall alerts)
  • District administration pages for road and permit information

Frequently asked questions

How many days do I need for Uttarakhand?

For one region, 5-7 days is comfortable. A single city or town can be done in 1-2.
