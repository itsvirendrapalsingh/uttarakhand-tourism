Trek finder

Filter Uttarakhand treks by difficulty, length, season and altitude

Home / Treks

Kedarkantha

A popular winter trek in the Govind Pashu Vihar area with pine forest, a frozen lake and a summit with wide views. Often chosen as a first snow trek.

Easy-moderate · 5 days · summit about 3,810 m

Har Ki Dun

A valley trek through the Supin river valley with traditional villages, forest and views at the head of a glacial valley. It is a longer and quieter trek than Kedarkantha.

Moderate · 7 days · about 3,500 m

Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Trek

A trek to a protected alpine valley known for its flowers, with an optional climb to Hemkund Sahib. The route is busy in season because many pilgrims walk it too.

Moderate · 6 days · Valley about 3,600 m; Hemkund about 4,300 m

Kuari Pass

A ridge trek along the 'Curzon trail' with long views of Nanda Devi, Dronagiri and other peaks. It starts from the Joshimath side and suits moderately fit trekkers.

Moderate · 5 days · pass about 3,860 m

Nag Tibba

A short weekend trek close to Dehradun and Mussoorie. A good first trek for beginners and families with fit children.

Easy · 2 days · summit about 3,000 m

Dayara Bugyal

A high meadow trek above Uttarkashi with broad open slopes and views of the Bandarpoonch and Gangotri ranges. Good for beginners who want a meadow trek.

Easy-moderate · 4 days · meadow about 3,400 m

Brahmatal

A winter trek in the Kumaon-Garhwal border area with frozen lakes, Mount Trishul views and an oak forest. Popular for snow in winter.

Moderate · 6 days · ridge about 3,800 m

Chopta-Tungnath-Chandrashila

A short trek to the Tungnath temple and on to the Chandrashila summit. It is one of the easiest ways to reach 4,000 m in Uttarakhand.

Easy · 3 days · Tungnath about 3,680 m; Chandrashila about 4,000 m

Pindari Glacier

A long valley trek in Kumaon to the Pindari glacier snout, with river valleys, villages and forest.

Moderate · 7 days · Zero Point about 3,600-3,700 m

Milam Glacier

A remote trek up the Johar valley to Milam village and the Milam glacier. Long days, a border area, and a permit are involved. Best for experienced trekkers with an operator.

Difficult · 9 days · Milam village about 3,400 m

Auden's Col

An advanced, high-altitude crossing between the Gangotri and Bhilangna valleys. It involves glacier travel and technical sections. Not for independent trekkers.

Difficult · 12 days · col about 5,500 m

Difficulty and season are general guidance. Always check current conditions and permits with a registered operator.
