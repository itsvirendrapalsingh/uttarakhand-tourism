Garhwal

The western half of Uttarakhand: the Char Dham shrines, Rishikesh and Haridwar, Mussoorie, and treks such as Kedarkantha and Har Ki Dun.

Home / Destinations / Garhwal

Yamunotri

Source shrine of the River Yamuna, first stop of the traditional Char Dham yatra

about 3,293 mUttarkashi

Gangotri

Temple of Goddess Ganga and gateway to the Gaumukh glacier

about 3,100 mUttarkashi

Kedarnath

Shiva shrine in the Mandakini valley, reached by a long uphill walk or by helicopter

about 3,583 mRudraprayag

Badrinath

Vishnu shrine on the Alaknanda, reachable by road

about 3,100 mChamoli

Dehradun

State capital and the main gateway to Mussoorie and the Garhwal hills

about 640 mDehradun

Mussoorie

Queen of the Hills, a colonial-era hill station above the Doon valley

about 2,000 mDehradun

Rishikesh

Yoga capital on the Ganga and gateway to the Char Dham routes

about 350 mDehradun and Tehri Garhwal

Haridwar

One of Hinduism's seven holiest cities, where the Ganga enters the plains

about 300 mHaridwar

New Tehri and Tehri Lake

A planned town above the Tehri reservoir, one of India's tallest dams

about 1,750 mTehri Garhwal

Kanatal

Quiet village with orchards, camps and views on the Mussoorie-Chamba road

about 2,500 mTehri Garhwal

Dhanaulti

Deodar and pine forests above Mussoorie, quieter and cooler

about 2,200-2,300 mTehri Garhwal

Chakrata

Cantonment hill town with forest, waterfalls and relatively few visitors

about 2,100 mDehradun

Uttarkashi

Bhagirathi valley town, gateway to Gangotri and several treks

about 1,150 mUttarkashi

Harsil and Dharali

High Bhagirathi valley villages with apple orchards and Himalayan views

about 2,600 mUttarkashi

Joshimath

Winter seat of Badrinath and the base for Auli and the Valley of Flowers

about 1,900 mChamoli

Auli

Himalayan ski slope with views of Nanda Devi and neighbouring peaks

about 2,500-3,000 mChamoli

Chopta and Tungnath

Forest meadows and the highest Shiva temple, with an easy short trek

Chopta about 2,700 m; Tungnath about 3,680 mRudraprayag

Devprayag

The confluence where the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi form the Ganga

about 475 mTehri Garhwal

Khirsu and Pauri

Quiet village above Pauri with orchards, forest and Himalayan views

about 1,700 mPauri Garhwal

Lansdowne

Quiet cantonment hill station with a colonial feel

about 1,700 mPauri Garhwal

Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Sahib

UNESCO World Heritage alpine valley, and the Sikh shrine of Hemkund Sahib

Valley about 3,600 m; Hemkund Sahib about 4,300 mChamoli

Mana Village

The last motorable village before the Tibet border area, a short drive from Badrinath

about 3,200 mChamoli

Rajaji National Park

Forest reserve near Haridwar and Rishikesh, known for elephants

about 300-1,000 mHaridwar, Dehradun and Pauri
