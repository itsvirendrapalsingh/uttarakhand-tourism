Kedarkantha

A popular winter trek in the Govind Pashu Vihar area with pine forest, a frozen lake and a summit with wide views. Often chosen as a first snow trek.

DifficultyEasy-moderate
Duration5 days
Distanceabout 20 km round trip
Altitudesummit about 3,810 m
SeasonDecember to April for snow

Highlights

  • Pine and oak forests in snow
  • Juda ka Talab lake
  • Summit panorama of Swargarohini and Bandarpoonch

Getting to the start

Sankri village; Dehradun to Sankri is a long mountain drive of roughly 190 km, usually 9-10 hours.

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1: Drive Dehradun to Sankri. Long road day via Purola. Arrive in the evening and stay in a homestay.
  2. Day 2: Sankri to Juda ka Talab. A few hours of walking through forest to a frozen lake campsite.
  3. Day 3: Juda ka Talab to Kedarkantha Base. Short walk to the base camp; acclimatise and rest.
  4. Day 4: Summit and descend to Hargaon. Early start for the summit, then a long descent. This is the longest day.
  5. Day 5: Hargaon to Sankri and drive back. Walk down to Sankri and drive back or stay another night.

Stay and facilities

Camping on the trek; homestays at Sankri.

Basic toilet tents on organised treks; no electricity or reliable network after Sankri.

Permits

Entry is controlled by the forest department; operators normally arrange fees. Confirm current permit rules.

What to carry

See the trek preparation and packing guide for day-hike, supported-trek and expedition lists.

Safety and acclimatisation

  • Do not trek independently in snow; use a registered operator and guide.
  • Carry layers and waterproof gloves.

Cost

Operator-run treks usually charge a package fee that includes transport from the base, food and guides. Get written quotes and inclusions. Budget estimator

Responsible trekking

  • Carry out all litter.
  • Do not leave the trail or camp outside designated spots.

Who it suits

  • First-time trekkers who are fit
  • Winter trekkers with a guide

Frequently asked questions

Can beginners do Kedarkantha?

Yes with a registered operator and good fitness. The summit day is long.
