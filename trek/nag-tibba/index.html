Nag Tibba

A short weekend trek close to Dehradun and Mussoorie. A good first trek for beginners and families with fit children.

Home / Treks / Nag Tibba

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel. Never trek high altitude alone or without a registered operator in snow.

Highlights

  • Weekend access from Dehradun
  • Forest campsite
  • Views of the Bandarpoonch range on clear days

Getting to the start

Pantwari village, roughly 85-90 km from Dehradun (about 3-4 hours).

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1: Pantwari to Nag Tibba base. A steady climb through forest to the campsite.
  2. Day 2: Summit and return. Early walk to the summit and descent to Pantwari.

Stay and facilities

Camps and homestays.

Basic; no network on the upper trail.

Permits

Check local forest rules; operators usually handle them.

What to carry

See the trek preparation and packing guide for day-hike, supported-trek and expedition lists.

Safety and acclimatisation

  • Do not trek in poor weather.
  • Carry water.

Cost

Short guided trips are cheaper than the multi-day treks; confirm inclusions. Budget estimator

Responsible trekking

  • Take litter down.

Who it suits

  • Beginners
  • Weekend trips from Dehradun or Delhi

Frequently asked questions

Can I do Nag Tibba in a day?

It can be done in a long day but a night at the campsite is more relaxed.
