Dayara Bugyal

A high meadow trek above Uttarkashi with broad open slopes and views of the Bandarpoonch and Gangotri ranges. Good for beginners who want a meadow trek.

Home / Treks / Dayara Bugyal

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel. Never trek high altitude alone or without a registered operator in snow.

Highlights

  • Wide alpine meadows
  • Snow in winter, flowers in summer
  • Views of major Himalayan ranges

Getting to the start

Barsu village near Raithal, about 30-35 km from Uttarkashi.

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1: Uttarkashi to Barsu. Drive to Barsu and rest.
  2. Day 2: Barsu to Gui Pass. A climb to the first campsite.
  3. Day 3: Gui to Dayara and back. Walk onto the meadow and return to camp.
  4. Day 4: Return to Barsu. Descent and drive back.

Stay and facilities

Camping and village homestays.

Limited; carry what you need.

Permits

Forest entry fees apply.

What to carry

See the trek preparation and packing guide for day-hike, supported-trek and expedition lists.

Safety and acclimatisation

  • Whiteout is possible in snow; do not leave the route.
  • Lightning is a risk on open meadows in monsoon.

Cost

Operator packages are widely available; compare inclusions. Budget estimator

Responsible trekking

  • Stay on established paths; the meadow is fragile.

Who it suits

  • Beginners
  • Families

Frequently asked questions

Can I see snow at Dayara?

Yes in winter and early spring; check conditions.
