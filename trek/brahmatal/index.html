Brahmatal

A winter trek in the Kumaon-Garhwal border area with frozen lakes, Mount Trishul views and an oak forest. Popular for snow in winter.

Duration6 days
Distanceabout 24-30 km
Altituderidge about 3,800 m

Highlights

  • Frozen lakes at Bekaltal and Brahmatal
  • Views of Mount Trishul and Nanda Ghunti
  • Forest walks

Getting to the start

Lohajung village, a long drive from Kathgodam or Haridwar.

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1: Reach Lohajung. Long drive via Gwaldam.
  2. Day 2: Lohajung to Bekaltal. Climb to the lake campsite.
  3. Day 3: Bekaltal to Brahmatal. Longer walk across the ridge.
  4. Day 4: Summit day. Climb to the ridge for views and return.
  5. Day 5: Descent to Lohajung. Walk out.
  6. Day 6: Drive back. Return.

Stay and facilities

Camping.

Minimal; carry everything.

Permits

Safety and acclimatisation

  • Do not trek without a guide in snow.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brahmatal good for beginners?

With a guide and good fitness, yes. Nights are cold.
