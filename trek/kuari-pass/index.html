Kuari Pass

A ridge trek along the 'Curzon trail' with long views of Nanda Devi, Dronagiri and other peaks. It starts from the Joshimath side and suits moderately fit trekkers.

Home / Treks / Kuari Pass

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel. Never trek high altitude alone or without a registered operator in snow.

Highlights

  • Panoramas of the Nanda Devi group
  • Oak and rhododendron forest
  • Gorson Bugyal link

Getting to the start

Dhak village near Joshimath.

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1: Joshimath to Gulling top. A steady climb to the first camp.
  2. Day 2: Gulling to Khullara. Ridge walk through forest.
  3. Day 3: Khullara to Kuari Pass. Climb to the pass and views.
  4. Day 4: Pass to Tali. Descent through forest and meadows.
  5. Day 5: Tali to Auli. Walk out near Auli and drive back.

Stay and facilities

Camping.

No facilities on the route; carry everything.

Permits

Check forest department requirements; operators usually arrange them.

Who it suits

Frequently asked questions

Is Kuari Pass good in winter?

Yes for snow, but trails can be icy and you need a guide.
