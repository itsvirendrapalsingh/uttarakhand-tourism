Pindari Glacier

A long valley trek in Kumaon to the Pindari glacier snout, with river valleys, villages and forest.

Highlights

  • Pindari river valley
  • Villages such as Khati
  • Views of Nanda Kot and Panwali Dwar

Getting to the start

Bageshwar, then a road to Song or Loharkhet.

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1: Bageshwar to Loharkhet/Dhakuri. Drive and short walk.
  2. Day 2: Dhakuri to Khati. Walk along the valley.
  3. Day 3: Khati to Dwali. Gradual climb along the river.
  4. Day 4: Dwali to Phurkia. Higher camp on the trail.
  5. Day 5: Phurkia to Zero Point and back. A long walk to the glacier.
  6. Day 6-7: Return. Descent via the same route.

Stay and facilities

  • Altitude gain is gradual but real.
