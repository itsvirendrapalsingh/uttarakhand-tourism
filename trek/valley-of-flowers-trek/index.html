Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Trek

A trek to a protected alpine valley known for its flowers, with an optional climb to Hemkund Sahib. The route is busy in season because many pilgrims walk it too.

Home / Treks / Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Trek

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel. Never trek high altitude alone or without a registered operator in snow.

Highlights

  • Flower meadows in July-August
  • Pushpawati river valley
  • Hemkund Sahib and lake

Getting to the start

Govindghat, about an hour from Joshimath by road.

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1: Reach Joshimath. Long drive from Rishikesh.
  2. Day 2: Govindghat to Ghangaria. About 13 km walk or pony ride on a steady path.
  3. Day 3: Valley of Flowers. Walk into the valley and return to Ghangaria the same day.
  4. Day 4: Hemkund Sahib. Steep climb from Ghangaria and return.
  5. Day 5: Ghangaria to Govindghat. Walk down and drive to Joshimath.

Stay and facilities

Lodges and the gurdwara at Ghangaria.

Shops, lodges and a small medical facility at Ghangaria in season; limited network.

Permits

Entry needs a permit, bought online or at the forest counter in Ghangaria. The park opened 1 June 2026 and was reported open to 31 October; entry is reported 7 am to 2 pm with exit by about 5 pm, and overnight stays in the valley are not allowed. Fees are reported inconsistently (confirm with the forest department).

Safety and acclimatisation

  • Landslides can block the Govindghat trail.
  • Do not stay in the valley after dark.

Cost

Costs depend on lodging and pony hire; check current rates locally. Budget estimator

Responsible trekking

  • Do not pick flowers.
  • Stay on the marked path.

Who it suits

  • Families with fit children
  • First-time trekkers
  • Pilgrims visiting Hemkund Sahib

When is the best bloom?

Usually July to August, but it varies year to year.
