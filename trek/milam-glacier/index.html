Milam Glacier

A remote trek up the Johar valley to Milam village and the Milam glacier. Long days, a border area, and a permit are involved. Best for experienced trekkers with an operator.

Duration9 days

Highlights

  • Remote Johar valley and Bhotiya villages
  • Milam glacier
  • Views of Hardeol and Trishuli peaks

Getting to the start

Munsiyari, then road and trail via Lilam.

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1-2: Reach Munsiyari. Long drive from Kathgodam or Pithoragarh.
  2. Day 3: Munsiyari to Lilam and Bogdiar. Start of the valley route.
  3. Day 4-6: Bogdiar to Martoli and Milam. Long walking days with villages.
  4. Day 7: Glacier day. Walk to the glacier and return.
  5. Day 8-9: Return. Descent via the same route.

Stay and facilities

KMVN huts and camps.

Very limited; no network on most of the route.

Permits

Reports conflict on the Inner Line Permit: one report says the ITBP stopped requiring it for tourists going up to Milam, while operators still list it. Confirm with the Munsiyari SDM office and carry ID and photos.

Safety and acclimatisation

  • Do not attempt unsupported.
