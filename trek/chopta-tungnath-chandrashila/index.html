Chopta-Tungnath-Chandrashila

A short trek to the Tungnath temple and on to the Chandrashila summit. It is one of the easiest ways to reach 4,000 m in Uttarakhand.

DifficultyEasy
Duration3 days
Distanceabout 10 km round trip from Chopta to Chandrashila
AltitudeTungnath about 3,680 m; Chandrashila about 4,000 m
SeasonMost of the year

Highlights

  • Tungnath temple
  • Summit sunrise views
  • Chopta meadows

Getting to the start

Chopta, with Tungnath about 3.5-4 km on foot.

Itinerary (typical)

  1. Day 1: Drive to Chopta. Long road day via Ukhimath.
  2. Day 2: Chopta to Tungnath and Chandrashila. Steady climb on a paved trail; return to Chopta.
  3. Day 3: Explore or leave. Short walks or begin the return.

Stay and facilities

Small hotels, camps and GMVN lodge.

Basic eateries at Tungnath and Chopta; limited network.

Permits

No special trek permit is typically needed; confirm locally.

What to carry

See the trek preparation and packing guide for day-hike, supported-trek and expedition lists.

Safety and acclimatisation

  • Snow hides the trail in winter.
  • Start early to avoid afternoon clouds.

Cost

No operator is strictly needed; guides are optional in summer. Budget estimator

Responsible trekking

  • Do not litter; plastic waste is a problem here.

Who it suits

  • Families
  • Beginners
  • Photographers

Map

Approximate area only. Do not navigate by this map.

Frequently asked questions

Is it open in winter?

The trail is open but snow-covered; the temple is closed.
