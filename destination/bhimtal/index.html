Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal

Larger, calmer lakes near Nainital

Altitudeabout 1,400 m

Bhimtal is a large lake with an island aquarium and lakeside cafes. Naukuchiatal, nearby, is a deep nine-cornered lake known for boating and paragliding. Both are calmer than Nainital.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Bhimtal Lake and island. Boating and a small aquarium on the island.
  • Naukuchiatal. Boating and paragliding with licensed operators.
  • Hidimba Parvat and nearby walks. Short forest walks.

How to reach Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal

By road

Bhimtal is about 22 km from Nainital and 25 km from Kathgodam by road.

By rail

Kathgodam.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

Lakeside hotels and homestays.

Food

Cafes and dhabas by the lake.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bhimtal better than Nainital?

It is calmer and less crowded but has fewer sights; many people combine both.
