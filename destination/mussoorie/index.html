Mussoorie

Queen of the Hills, a colonial-era hill station above the Doon valley

Altitudeabout 2,000 m
Best timeMarch to June and September to November

Mussoorie sits on a ridge about 35 km from Dehradun with views of the Doon valley and, on clear days, snow peaks. It is the most visited hill station in Garhwal and gets very crowded on weekends and holidays. Quieter stays are in Landour and nearby Dhanaulti.

Things to see

  • Mall Road. Main promenade with shops, cafes and views. Busiest in the evening.
  • Gun Hill. Viewpoint reached by a short ropeway or walk.
  • Kempty Falls. Popular waterfall about 15 km out; very crowded in summer.
  • Camel's Back Road. Easy 3 km walk, good in the morning.
  • Landour. Quiet, old cantonment area with cafes and short walks. Good for a slower stay.

How to reach Mussoorie

By road

Dehradun to Mussoorie is about 35 km, usually 1.5-2 hours. Weekend queues on the access road can add a lot.

By rail

Dehradun is the railhead; taxis and shared cabs run to Mussoorie.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road via Dehradun.

Where to stay

Hotels cluster near Mall Road and the Library area. For quiet, look at Landour or properties outside the main town.

Food

Cafes in Landour and Mall Road. Trying fresh bun-omelettes and chai at roadside stalls is a local habit.

Season and weather

Pleasant summers; heavy rain and fog in July-August; cold winters with occasional snow.

Tips

  • Avoid weekend arrivals in May-June if you can; traffic is heavy.
  • Vehicle entry to Mall Road is restricted, so plan to walk.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mussoorie good for snow?

It sometimes gets light snow in winter but it is not reliable. Dhanaulti and Chakrata are likelier to see snow.
