Devprayag

The confluence where the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi form the Ganga

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Devprayag

Devprayag is the sacred confluence where the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi join to become the Ganga. It is on the Rishikesh-Badrinath road and is a natural stop for a break.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Sangam viewpoint. Viewing area where the two rivers meet.
  • Raghunath Temple. Old temple above the confluence.

How to reach Devprayag

By road

About 70 km from Rishikesh on the NH-7, usually 2-2.5 hours.

By rail

Rishikesh, then road.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Few options; most visitors do not stay overnight.

Food

Roadside dhabas.

Budget

Season and weather

Rivers are strong and muddy in monsoon; take care near the edge.

Tips

  • Plan a tea or lunch halt here on Char Dham or Kedarnath routes.
