Harsil and Dharali

High Bhagirathi valley villages with apple orchards and Himalayan views

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Harsil and Dharali

Harsil is a small village on the road to Gangotri, with deodar forest, the Bhagirathi river and a military presence. Dharali, nearby, is a village on the road. A major flash flood and debris flow hit Dharali and Harsil on 5 August 2025; the Uttarkashi-Harsil road was restored by late August 2025 but the Gangotri road near Harsil was damaged. We could not confirm 2026 status, so check current road and lodging conditions.

About this page: The 5 August 2025 flash flood is confirmed by multiple news reports; 2026 road and lodging status could not be confirmed. Other details are approximate and from general knowledge and secondary sources.

Things to see

  • Bhagirathi riverside walks. Easy walks near the village.
  • Mukhba. Village where the Gangotri deity stays in winter.
  • Orchards. Apple orchards around the village.

How to reach Harsil and Dharali

By road

About 70-75 km from Uttarkashi on the Gangotri road; Gangotri is roughly 25 km further.

By rail

Rishikesh is the railhead.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Homestays and small guesthouses; limited rooms and basic facilities.

Food

Simple meals at guesthouses.

Budget

Season and weather

Closed in parts by snow in winter; monsoon landslides.

Safety

  • Check road and lodging status before you go. Dharali and Harsil were hit by a flash flood on 5 August 2025.

Tips

  • Carry cash and a power bank; services are limited.

Frequently asked questions

Is Harsil open all year?

The village is inhabited year-round but winter snow can block the road and close many lodges.
