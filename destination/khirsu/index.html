Khirsu and Pauri

Quiet village above Pauri with orchards, forest and Himalayan views

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Khirsu and Pauri

Khirsu is a small village about 15 km from Pauri town, with apple orchards and deodar forest. It is relatively quiet with a handful of homestays and a few hotels. Pauri offers a town base with views of the snow peaks on clear days.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Orchards and village walks. Easy walks through orchards and forest.
  • Kyunkaleshwar Temple (Pauri). Hill temple above Pauri.
  • Himalayan viewpoints. Views of snow peaks on clear days.

How to reach Khirsu and Pauri

By road

Khirsu is about 15 km from Pauri; Pauri is about 110-130 km from Rishikesh by road via Srinagar or Devprayag, around 4-5 hours.

By rail

Rishikesh or Kotdwar, then taxi.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Homestays and small resorts; book ahead in season.

Food
