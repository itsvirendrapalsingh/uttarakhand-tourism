Jageshwar

A forest valley with a group of old stone temples

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Jageshwar

Jageshwar is a valley of deodar forest with a large cluster of old stone temples, many dedicated to Shiva. It is a short drive from Almora and is calm, especially outside festival days.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Jageshwar temple complex. Group of ancient stone temples in a deodar grove.
  • Vriddha Jageshwar. A hilltop temple above the valley, reached by a short drive and a walk.
  • Dandeshwar Temple. A larger temple nearby on the same road.

How to reach Jageshwar

By road

About 35 km from Almora by road, roughly 1 hour.

By rail

Kathgodam.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

A few lodges at Jageshwar; most visitors stay in Almora or Binsar.

Food

Simple dhabas.

Tips
