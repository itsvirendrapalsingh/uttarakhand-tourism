Pithoragarh

Eastern Kumaon town in a wide valley, gateway to Munsiyari and the Kailash Mansarovar route

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Pithoragarh

Pithoragarh sits in the Soar valley and is the main town of eastern Kumaon. It is the usual halt on the way to Munsiyari and Dharchula, and an administrative centre for the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra on the Lipulekh route.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Pithoragarh Fort. Old fort with views of the valley.
  • Chandak. Hill viewpoint above the town.
  • Local markets. Handicrafts and woollens.

How to reach Pithoragarh

By road

About 200 km from Kathgodam via Almora, a long day of driving.

By rail

Tanakpur or Kathgodam, then road.

By air

Naini Saini airstrip service is limited; confirm current flights.

Where to stay

Hotels in town; limited elsewhere.

Food

Local eateries.
