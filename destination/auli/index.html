Auli

Himalayan ski slope with views of Nanda Devi and neighbouring peaks

Altitudeabout 2,500-3,000 m
Best timeJanuary to March for snow
Suggested stay1 to 2 nights
RegionGarhwal

Auli is a meadow-and-ski area above Joshimath, with long slopes and views of the Nanda Devi group on clear days. Skiing depends on snowfall, usually between January and March. In summer it is a green meadow and a base for treks such as Gorson Bugyal.

About this page: Ropeway status comes from a PTI report (April 2025); no official 2026 notice was found. Other details are approximate and from general knowledge and secondary sources.

Things to see

  • Ski slopes. Skiing season depends on snowfall; lessons and gear are available through local operators.
  • Gorson Bugyal. Meadow trek from Auli.
  • Artificial lake. A snow-making reservoir; closed or empty in some months.
  • Cable car. Joshimath-Auli ropeway; reported suspended as of April 2025 (PTI). Confirm current status.

How to reach Auli

By road

About 15 km from Joshimath by a mountain road, or by the ropeway if it is running. Snow can block the Joshimath-Auli road in winter.

By rail

Rishikesh, then a long road journey via Joshimath.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

GMVN and private hotels; book early for ski season.

Food

Hotel meals; limited eateries.

Heavy snow can close the road; carry chains if driving.

Tips

  • Confirm that snow is on the slopes before booking a ski trip.
  • Check Joshimath advisories before travelling.
