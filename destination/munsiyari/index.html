Munsiyari

Remote hill town facing the Panchachuli peaks, base for Johar valley treks

Munsiyari is a remote hill town in the Johar valley, with a front-row view of the Panchachuli peaks. It is a base for treks to Milam, Ralam and Khaliya Top. The journey is long, which keeps crowds relatively low.

About this page: Inner Line Permit status for Milam is reported inconsistently and was not confirmed by an official source. Travel times are approximate.

Things to see

  • Panchachuli views. Viewpoints in the town and at Khaliya Top.
  • Khaliya Top. Meadow trek above Munsiyari.
  • Birthi Falls. Waterfall on the road to Munsiyari.
  • Tribal Heritage Museum. Collection on the Johar valley's Bhotiya heritage.

How to reach Munsiyari

By road

About 130 km from Pithoragarh, and roughly 290-300 km from Kathgodam; plan on a long day of driving (typically 10 hours or more).

By rail

Kathgodam or Tanakpur.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

KMVN rest house, hotels and homestays; limited rooms in season.

Food

Local meals including mountain rajma; few restaurants.

Budget

Season and weather

Cold winters; monsoon landslides on the approach road.

Tips

  • Break the journey at Almora or Pithoragarh.
  • Network and ATMs are limited; carry cash.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for Munsiyari?

Not for the town itself. For the Milam trek, reports conflict: one report says the ITBP stopped requiring an Inner Line Permit, while operators still list it. Check with the Munsiyari SDM office.
