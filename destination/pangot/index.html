Pangot and Kilbury

Birdwatching village a short drive from Nainital

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Pangot and Kilbury

Pangot is a small village about 15 km from Nainital, known for forest birdwatching. Kilbury, nearby, has oak and pine forest and a few quiet lodges. It is a quiet alternative to Nainital.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Birdwatching. Early morning walks with local guides.
  • Forest walks. Walks through oak and pine forest.

How to reach Pangot and Kilbury

By road

About 15 km from Nainital; roughly 40 minutes by road.
