Kausani

Ridge village with wide views of the Himalayan peaks

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Kausani

Kausani is a small village on a ridge with a wide view of the Himalayan range, including Trishul and Nanda Devi on clear days. It has Gandhi's Anasakti Ashram and a tea estate. It suits a relaxed stay of one or two nights.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Himalayan sunrise views. Best in clear weather, especially in autumn and winter.
  • Anasakti Ashram. Where Mahatma Gandhi stayed and wrote on the Gita.
  • Uttarakhand Tea Estate. Tea garden with tasting.
  • Baijnath. Group of old temples about 20 km away.

How to reach Kausani

By road

About 120 km from Kathgodam, 4-5 hours; roughly 55 km from Almora.

By rail

Kathgodam.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

Resorts and hotels along the ridge; book ahead for peak season.

Food

Hotel restaurants and local eateries.

Season and weather

Clear views most likely October to February; monsoon clouds.

Tips

  • Wake early for views; haze often builds by mid-morning.
