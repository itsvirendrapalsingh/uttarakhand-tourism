Dhanaulti

Deodar and pine forests above Mussoorie, quieter and cooler

Dhanaulti is a small hill village about 25-30 km from Mussoorie, set in deodar and rhododendron forest. It offers walks and eco parks and sees more snow than Mussoorie in winter.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Eco Parks. Forest department parks with walkways; check entry fees and timings.
  • Surkanda Devi Temple. A short climb from the road at Kaddukhal.
  • Forest walks. Walks through deodar forest near the village.

How to reach Dhanaulti

By road

About 25-30 km from Mussoorie by road; the drive from Dehradun is 2-3 hours depending on the route.

By rail

Dehradun is the railhead.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Resorts and homestays along the Mussoorie-Chamba road; fewer options than Mussoorie.

Food

Resort restaurants and small dhabas.

Season and weather

Snow is possible between December and February. Roads can become slippery; check conditions.

Tips

  • In snow, hire a driver used to mountain roads.
  • Carry cash for small shops.
