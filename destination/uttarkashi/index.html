Uttarkashi

Bhagirathi valley town, gateway to Gangotri and several treks

Uttarkashi is the main town on the Gangotri road. It has the Kashi Vishwanath temple and the Nehru Institute of Mountaineering. It is the practical halt before Gangotri and a base for Dayara Bugyal, Dodital and other treks.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Vishwanath Temple. Old temple in the town centre.
  • Nehru Institute of Mountaineering. Mountaineering institute; check access rules for visitors.
  • Treks nearby. Dayara Bugyal (via Barsu), Dodital and the Gangotri road treks.

How to reach Uttarkashi

By road

About 170 km from Rishikesh via Chamba, usually 6-8 hours depending on road conditions.

By rail

Rishikesh or Dehradun are the railheads.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Several hotels and guesthouses; the best choice on the route to Gangotri.

Food

Hotel and dhaba meals.

Season and weather

The road can be disrupted by monsoon landslides.

Tips

  • Fill fuel and withdraw cash here before going on to Gangotri.
  • Book permits for Gaumukh through the forest department in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Is Uttarkashi a good overnight stop?

Yes. It is the largest town on the route to Gangotri and has the most hotels.
