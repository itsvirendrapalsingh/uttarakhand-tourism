Mana Village

The last motorable village before the Tibet border area, a short drive from Badrinath

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Mana Village

Mana is a village about 3 km from Badrinath, home to the Bhotiya community. It is the last settlement on this road. Visitors come for Vyas Gufa, Ganesh Gufa, Bhim Pul and the walk to Vasudhara falls.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Vyas Gufa and Ganesh Gufa. Caves linked to the Mahabharata and Ganesh.
  • Bhim Pul. Natural rock bridge over the Saraswati river.
  • Vasudhara Falls. Waterfall reached by a walk beyond Mana; check trail conditions.

How to reach Mana Village

By road

About 3 km from Badrinath by road.

By rail

Rishikesh, then road.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

No major lodging in Mana; stay in Badrinath or Joshimath.

Food

Small tea shops serve local snacks.

Tips

  • Visit after your Badrinath darshan on the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a permit for Mana?

Visitors normally need only ID for the village. Treks beyond may need permits; check locally.
