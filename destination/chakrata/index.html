Chakrata

Cantonment hill town with forest, waterfalls and relatively few visitors

Chakrata is a quiet town in the Jaunsar-Bawar area. It is a military cantonment, so some areas are restricted. Visitors come for Tiger Falls, forest walks and the Deoban deodar forest.

About this page: Access rules for foreign nationals come from news and travel guides, not an official notice; confirm with the district administration. Other details are approximate and from general knowledge and secondary sources.

Things to see

  • Tiger Falls. A tall waterfall reached by a drive and a walk; best after the monsoon but check conditions.
  • Deoban. Deodar forest with views; roads can be rough.
  • Chilmiri Neck. Sunset viewpoint near the town.
  • Lakhamandal. Ancient temple site on the Yamuna, a long drive from Chakrata.

How to reach Chakrata

By road

About 90-100 km from Dehradun via Vikasnagar, typically 3-4 hours. Roads are narrow in places.

By rail

Dehradun is the railhead.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Small hotels, homestays and a GMVN-style rest house; limited choice, so book in advance.

Food

Simple eateries and hotel meals.

Season and weather

Cold winters with snow possible; monsoon brings landslides.

Safety

  • Parts of the area are controlled by the military and ID is checked at barriers. Carry ID and obey signage.
  • Foreign nationals are reported to be barred from entering, or to need permission from security authorities (sources name different offices). Check with the Dehradun district administration before planning.

Tips

  • Fill fuel and withdraw cash in Vikasnagar before climbing.
  • Do not photograph military installations.

Frequently asked questions

Are there entry rules for Chakrata?

Some areas are restricted because of the cantonment, and ID is checked on the road. Foreign nationals are reported to be barred or to need a permit; confirm with the district administration.
