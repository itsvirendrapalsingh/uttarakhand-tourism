Haridwar

One of Hinduism's seven holiest cities, where the Ganga enters the plains

Haridwar is a major pilgrimage city on the Ganga and the main railhead for Rishikesh and the Char Dham routes. The evening aarti at Har Ki Pauri is the signature experience. It is very crowded on festival days.

About this page: The 2027 Ardh Kumbh dates come from news reports (PTI) and were not checked against the mela administration. Distances are approximate. Other details are approximate and from general knowledge and secondary sources.

Things to see

  • Har Ki Pauri aarti. Evening Ganga aarti at dusk. Arrive early; the ghat fills up.
  • Mansa Devi Temple. Hill temple reached by ropeway or on foot.
  • Chandi Devi Temple. Hilltop temple across the river, reached by ropeway or trek.
  • Maya Devi Temple. One of the older temples in the city.

How to reach Haridwar

By road

About 200 km from Delhi, typically 4-5 hours; Rishikesh is about 25 km north.

By rail

Haridwar Junction has frequent trains from Delhi and other major cities.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Hotels and dharamshalas around Har Ki Pauri, Bhupatwala and the railway station area. Book ahead for festival dates.

Food

Largely vegetarian local food; sweets such as kachori-sabzi and lassi are common breakfasts.

Season and weather

Festivals bring large crowds. The Ardh Kumbh at Haridwar is reported (PTI, April 2026) to run from 14 January to 20 April 2027, with bathing days including 14 January, 6 February, 20 February, 6 March, 8 March, 14 April and 20 April. Expect very heavy crowds and traffic controls; confirm the schedule with the mela administration. The Kanwar Yatra in the month of Shravan (July-August) also brings heavy crowds and traffic restrictions.

Safety

  • Hold on to bags and phones in crowded ghats.
  • Do not swim at the ghats during high flow.

Tips

  • Book hotels near Har Ki Pauri early for festival dates.
  • Check road diversions during the Kanwar Yatra.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Kumbh Mela at Haridwar?

It is held every 12 years, with an Ardh Kumbh at the midpoint. The 2027 Ardh Kumbh is reported to run from 14 January to 20 April 2027; confirm exact bathing dates with the mela administration.
