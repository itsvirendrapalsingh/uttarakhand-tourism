New Tehri and Tehri Lake

A planned town above the Tehri reservoir, one of India's tallest dams

Altitudeabout 1,750 m
Best timeSeptember to June
Suggested stay1 day or 1 night

New Tehri was built to replace old Tehri, which was submerged by the dam. The huge reservoir supports water sports and lakeside stays. It is about 75-80 km from Rishikesh by road and works as a day trip or a night stop.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Tehri Dam and reservoir. Viewpoints near the dam. Photography and entry rules apply; check on site.
  • Water sports and boating. Run by licensed operators on the reservoir. Season and availability vary.
  • Surkanda Devi (nearby). Hilltop temple on the Dhanaulti side, reached by a short climb.

How to reach New Tehri and Tehri Lake

By road

About 75-80 km from Rishikesh via Narendra Nagar and Chamba, about 2.5-3 hours; allow extra for mountain roads.

By rail

Rishikesh or Dehradun are the railheads.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Lakeside resorts and hotels in New Tehri and Koti Colony; camps on the shore in season.

Food

Hotel restaurants and dhabas; limited choice outside the town.

Budget

Season and weather

Winters are cold; monsoon rains cause landslides on connecting roads.

Tips

  • Confirm water-sport operations before you travel; reservoir levels vary through the year.
  • Combine with Kanatal or Dhanaulti for a hill-station add-on.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit Tehri Lake as a day trip from Rishikesh?

Yes, but the drive is long (2.5-3 hours each way), so an overnight stay is more relaxed.
