Lansdowne

Quiet cantonment hill station with a colonial feel

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Lansdowne

Lansdowne is a small cantonment town built by the British, now home to the Garhwal Rifles. It is clean, quiet and has a few short sights. Kotdwar is the nearest railhead.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Garhwal Rifles Regimental Museum. Museum of the regiment's history.
  • Bhulla Tal. Small lake maintained by the army.
  • St. Mary's Church. Old church in the cantonment.

How to reach Lansdowne

By road

About 40 km from Kotdwar; Delhi to Kotdwar is roughly 5 hours by road.

By rail

Kotdwar railway station.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport is far; most visitors drive from Delhi or Haridwar.

Where to stay

Small hotels and GMVN lodge.

Food
