Binsar is a forest sanctuary with oak and rhododendron forest, a hill viewpoint, and a handful of resorts. Entry to the sanctuary has an official fee and timings.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Zero Point viewpoint. Viewpoint inside the sanctuary with wide views.
  • Forest walks and birding. Walks through oak and rhododendron forest.
  • Binsar Mahadev. Old temple deeper in the forest.

How to reach Binsar

By road

About 30 km from Almora; the last section is a narrow forest road.

By rail

Kathgodam.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

Resorts around the sanctuary and some homestays.

Food

Resort restaurants.

Budget

Season and weather

Cold winters with occasional snow.

Safety

  • Follow sanctuary rules; do not leave marked trails.
  • Wild animals including leopards live in the area; do not walk alone at night.

Tips

  • Carry cash for entry fees; check timings with the forest office.
