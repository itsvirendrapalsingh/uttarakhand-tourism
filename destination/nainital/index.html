Nainital

Lake town around the pear-shaped Naini Lake

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Nainital

Nainital is built around Naini Lake and is among the most popular hill stations in Kumaon. It has boating, viewpoints and a busy Mall Road, and it gets very crowded in May-June. It is a good base for the nearby lakes and hill villages.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Naini Lake. Boating on the lake; the Mall Road runs along its shore.
  • Naina Devi Temple. Temple at the northern edge of the lake.
  • Snow View Point. Viewpoint reached by cable car or walk; views depend on the weather.
  • Tiffin Top. Walk to a viewpoint above the town.
  • Mall Road and Tibetan market. Shopping and street food.

How to reach Nainital

By road

About 300 km from Delhi, 6-7 hours in normal traffic via Haldwani and Kathgodam. Vehicle entry into the core of the town is restricted at peak times.

By rail

Kathgodam station, about 35 km from Nainital, with trains from Delhi and other cities.

By air

Pantnagar Airport has limited flights; check current schedules.

Where to stay

Hotels around Mall Road and Tallital. For a quieter stay, look at Bhimtal, Sattal or Pangot.

Food

Cafes on Mall Road, Tibetan food and local sweets.

Season and weather

Peak crowds in May-June and winter holidays. Monsoon rain causes landslides on approach roads.

Tips

  • Book hotels well ahead for May-June.
  • The approach road can have long queues on weekends.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do I need in Nainital?

Two nights covers the main sights; add a day for Bhimtal and Sattal.
