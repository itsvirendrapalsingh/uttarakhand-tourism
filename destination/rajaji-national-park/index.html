Rajaji National Park

Forest reserve near Haridwar and Rishikesh, known for elephants

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Rajaji National Park

Rajaji is a large protected forest spread across Haridwar, Dehradun and Pauri districts. It is known for its elephant population and offers jeep safaris from a few gateways such as Chilla and Motichur. It is an easy add-on from Haridwar or Rishikesh.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Jeep safari. Run through the park's gateways. Sightings are never guaranteed.
  • Chilla range. Near Haridwar and Rishikesh, a common safari entry.

How to reach Rajaji National Park

By road

Chilla gate is close to Haridwar (about 10-15 km); Rishikesh is nearby.

By rail

Haridwar.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport.

Where to stay

Hotels in Haridwar and Rishikesh, plus eco camps near the gates.

Food

Frequently asked questions

Will I see an elephant?

Possibly, but sightings are never guaranteed. Dawn and dusk give the best chances.
