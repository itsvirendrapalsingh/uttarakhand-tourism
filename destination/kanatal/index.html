Kanatal

Quiet village with orchards, camps and views on the Mussoorie-Chamba road

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Kanatal

Kanatal is a small hill village between Mussoorie and Chamba. It has camps, resorts and forest walks and is much quieter than Mussoorie, though weekends in peak season fill up. A good first stop for people who want mountain air without a busy town.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Surkanda Devi Temple. Hilltop temple a short climb from the road; views on clear days.
  • Forest and ridge walks. Easy walks through pine and oak forest.
  • Camping and adventure activities. Run by camps and resorts; check operators for safety standards.

How to reach Kanatal

By road

About 75-80 km from Dehradun (about 2.5-3 hours); about 85 km from Rishikesh.

By rail

Dehradun or Rishikesh, then taxi.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Resorts, camps and homestays around the village. Book ahead for weekends.

Food

Resort meals; limited eateries in the village.

Season and weather

Warm days, cool nights in summer; cold winters; monsoon fog and landslides.

Tips

  • Carry cash and a charged phone; services are patchy in places.
  • Weekdays are far quieter than weekends.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kanatal quiet?

Relatively quiet on weekdays and off-season. Weekends and holidays are busier.
