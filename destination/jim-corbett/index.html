Jim Corbett National Park

India's oldest national park, famous for tigers and elephants

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Jim Corbett National Park

Jim Corbett National Park, set up in 1936, is one of India's oldest national parks. It is a tiger reserve with several safari zones and forest rest houses. Tiger sightings are never guaranteed. Per the reserve's official site, Jhirna, Dhela and Garjia zones are open throughout the year, Bijrani from 15 October to 30 June, and Dhikala, Durgadevi, Sonanadi and Pakhro from 15 November to 15 June.

About this page: Zone opening windows, booking sites, ID and phone rules were checked against the reserve's official site on 2026-10-25. Travel times are approximate.

Things to see

  • Jeep safari. Run in zones such as Bijrani, Jhirna, Dhikala and others. Zone availability and timings change by season.
  • Dhikala. Forest zone with overnight stay in the park; reservation is needed.
  • Corbett Falls. Waterfall near the park.

How to reach Jim Corbett National Park

By road

About 240-260 km from Delhi, 5-6 hours via Moradabad to Ramnagar.

By rail

Ramnagar station has trains from Delhi.

By air

Pantnagar Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Resorts in Ramnagar, Dhikala and Garjia; government forest rest houses inside the park.

Food

Resort and rest house meals.

Season and weather

Zone windows from the official site: Jhirna, Dhela and Garjia all year; Bijrani 15 October to 30 June; Dhikala, Durgadevi, Sonanadi and Pakhro 15 November to 15 June. Confirm exact dates before planning.

Safety

  • Do not leave the vehicle inside the park unless instructed.
  • Wild elephants and tigers; follow the guide.

Tips

  • Book only on the official site: jeep safaris at booking.corbettgov.org and night stays at stays.corbettgov.org. The reserve warns of scam booking sites. A valid ID uploaded at booking must match at the gate, and mobile phones are not permitted inside tourism zones.
  • Do not promise yourself a tiger sighting; many safaris see none.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a Corbett safari at the gate?

The official site says the last online booking time is 6:30 am for the morning session and 4 pm for the afternoon session on the visit date. Book in advance and use only the official portal.
