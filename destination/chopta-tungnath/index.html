Chopta and Tungnath

Forest meadows and the highest Shiva temple, with an easy short trek

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Chopta and Tungnath

Chopta is a small settlement in rhododendron and oak forest. From here an easy trek leads to Tungnath, among the highest Shiva temples, and onward to Chandrashila. The temple closes in winter. Many call Chopta the 'mini Switzerland' of Uttarakhand.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Tungnath Temple. About 3.5-4 km walk from Chopta on a paved path.
  • Chandrashila summit. Short climb beyond Tungnath for wide views, around 4,000 m.
  • Deoria Tal. Small lake reached from Sari village, with a reflection of the Chaukhamba peaks on clear days.

How to reach Chopta and Tungnath

By road

Rishikesh to Chopta is about 220 km via Rudraprayag and Ukhimath, usually 8-9 hours; allow time for mountain roads.

By rail

Rishikesh or Haridwar are the railheads.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Small hotels, camps and a GMVN lodge at Chopta; more choice at Ukhimath, Sari and Duggalbitta.

Food

Basic dhabas and camp meals.

Season and weather

Snow in winter; the Tungnath temple is closed in winter.

Safety

  • Snow can hide the trail; hire a local guide in winter.
  • Altitude symptoms are possible at 3,500 m and above.

Tips

  • Start the Tungnath walk early; clouds roll in by midday.
  • Carry rain gear even in summer.

Frequently asked questions

How hard is the Tungnath walk?

It is a short, steady climb on a paved trail, suitable for most fit beginners. Allow longer if you go on to Chandrashila.
