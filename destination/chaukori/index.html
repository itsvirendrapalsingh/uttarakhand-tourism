Chaukori

Quiet village with Himalayan views on the way to Munsiyari

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Chaukori

Chaukori is a small village with wide Himalayan views and a tea estate. It is a quiet stop between Almora and Munsiyari and has a few homestays and resorts.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Himalayan viewpoints. Views of the Panchachuli and Nanda Devi range on clear days.
  • Tea estate. Small tea garden with walks.
  • Berinag. Small town nearby, known for its tea gardens.

How to reach Chaukori

By road

A long drive: roughly 8 hours or more from Kathgodam via Almora and Berinag, depending on road conditions.

By rail

Kathgodam.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

KMVN rest house and small resorts.

Food

Resort meals.
