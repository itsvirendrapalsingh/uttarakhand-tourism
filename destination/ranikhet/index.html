Ranikhet

Calm cantonment hill station with pine forests and Himalayan views

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Ranikhet

Ranikhet is a quiet cantonment town, base of the Kumaon Regiment, with pine forests and views of the snow peaks. It is calmer than Nainital. Note: there is no heritage toy train to Ranikhet, so plan on road travel from Kathgodam.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Chaubatia Gardens. Orchards and a garden area outside the town.
  • Jhula Devi Temple. Temple with a large number of bells.
  • Kumaon Regiment museum. Museum of the regiment's history.
  • Golf course. A well-known hilltop course.

How to reach Ranikhet

By road

About 80 km from Kathgodam, 3 hours or more; Nainital is about 60 km.

By rail

Kathgodam.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

Hotels, resorts and KMVN accommodation.

Food

Frequently asked questions

Is there a train to Ranikhet?

No. The nearest railhead is Kathgodam, and you continue by road.
