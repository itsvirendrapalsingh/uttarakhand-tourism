Joshimath

Winter seat of Badrinath and the base for Auli and the Valley of Flowers

Home / Destinations / Garhwal / Joshimath

Joshimath is the main halt on the road to Badrinath and the base for Auli and the Valley of Flowers trek via Govindghat. The town has been affected by land subsidence since early 2023. Check official advisories, lodging availability and ropeway status before planning.

About this page: Ropeway status comes from a PTI report (April 2025); no official 2026 notice was found. Other details are approximate and from general knowledge and secondary sources.

Things to see

  • Narsingh Temple. Temple where the Badrinath deity is worshipped in winter.
  • Auli ropeway. Cable car to Auli. A PTI report from April 2025 said it had been suspended for safety reasons; we found no official 2026 notice, so confirm before planning around it.
  • Base for Valley of Flowers and Hemkund. Govindghat is about an hour's drive from Joshimath.

How to reach Joshimath

By road

About 250 km from Rishikesh via Devprayag, Srinagar, Karnaprayag and Chamoli. Plan a full day; landslides can delay.

By rail

Rishikesh is the railhead.

By air

Jolly Grant Airport, then road.

Where to stay

Hotels and guesthouses, but availability has been affected by subsidence. Pipalkoti and Auli are alternatives.

Food

Dhabas and hotel meals.

Season and weather

Winters bring snow at Auli; monsoon landslides on the highway.

Safety

  • Check current subsidence and road advisories.
  • Do not assume any specific building or ropeway is open without checking.

Tips

  • Consider staying in Pipalkoti or Auli if Joshimath lodging is limited.
