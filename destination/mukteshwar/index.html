Mukteshwar

High ridge village with orchards and wide Himalayan views

Mukteshwar is a small, quiet ridge town above Nainital, known for its Shiva temple, fruit orchards and views of the Himalayan range. It has rock-climbing options and a research institute.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Mukteshwar Dham. Shiva temple on a hilltop with views.
  • Chauli Ki Jali. Rock formation and viewpoint with a cliff-edge walk.
  • Orchard walks. Apple and pear orchards around the village.

How to reach Mukteshwar

By road

About 50 km from Nainital via Bhowali and Ramgarh; roughly 2 hours.

By rail

Kathgodam.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

Resorts and homestays; book ahead in season.

Food

Resort restaurants; a few dhabas.
