Sattal

A cluster of connected lakes in an oak forest, popular with birdwatchers

Home / Destinations / Kumaon / Sattal

Sattal means seven lakes; the lakes sit in oak forest and are a quiet place for birdwatching and walks. Several lodges and camps offer stays near the water.

About this page: Compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources. Distances and times are approximate and were not checked against an official page. Confirm road, permit and opening status before you travel.

Things to see

  • Lake walks. Short forest paths linking the lakes.
  • Birdwatching. Resident and migratory birds; early morning is best.
  • Boating. Available on some lakes in season.

How to reach Sattal

By road

About 23 km from Nainital and roughly 20 km from Bhimtal.

By rail

Kathgodam.

By air

Pantnagar Airport.

Where to stay

Resorts and camps near the lakes; fewer options than Nainital.

Food

Resort and camp meals.

Season and weather

Pleasant for most of the year; monsoon fog.

Tips

  • Carry a hat and binoculars; avoid loud noise near the water.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sattal quiet?

It is far quieter than Nainital, though holiday weekends bring visitors.
