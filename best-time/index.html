Best time to visit Uttarakhand

Month by month: weather, snow, roads, treks and Char Dham status

Home / Best time to visit

These are typical seasonal patterns, not forecasts. For current weather use the India Meteorological Department and local advisories. Temperatures are described in general terms; no figures are quoted.

January

Weather
Cold in the hills, with freezing nights above about 2,000 m; plains are cool and foggy.
Snow
Snow likely in higher areas such as Auli, Chopta and Dhanaulti; Mukteshwar and Nainital may see some.
Roads
Some high roads may be closed after snowfall.
Trekking
Winter treks like Kedarkantha, Brahmatal and Dayara Bugyal.
Char Dham
Closed.
Wildlife
Corbett is open in some zones; birdwatching is good.
Festivals
Makar Sankranti and Uttarayani fairs. The Haridwar Ardh Kumbh is reported to begin on 14 January 2027.
Crowds
Low except around Auli and winter holidays.
Good picks
Auli, Dehradun, Rishikesh, Haridwar

February

Weather
Cold, with gradually warming days.
Snow
Snow remains in higher areas.
Roads
Mostly open; some high roads closed.
Trekking
Winter treks continue.
Char Dham
Closed.
Wildlife
Good for birds.
Festivals
Basant Panchami.
Crowds
Low to moderate.
Good picks
Rishikesh, Haridwar, Auli, Jim Corbett National Park

March

Weather
Spring begins; rhododendrons begin to bloom in the mid-hills.
Snow
Snow melts at mid altitudes.
Roads
Open.
Trekking
Spring treks begin.
Char Dham
Closed; preparations underway.
Wildlife
Good for most parks.
Festivals
Holi, including Kumaoni Baithaki Holi; the International Yoga Festival in Rishikesh is typically held around this time.
Crowds
Moderate.
Good picks
Rishikesh, Nainital, Bhimtal and Naukuchiatal, Sattal

April

Weather
Pleasant in the hills, hot in the plains.
Snow
Higher passes retain snow.
Roads
Open; Char Dham roads are readied.
Trekking
Many treks are in season.
Char Dham
Yamunotri and Gangotri typically open around Akshaya Tritiya; Kedarnath and Badrinath open late April or May.
Wildlife
Good.
Festivals
Baisakhi.
Crowds
Rising.
Good picks
Mussoorie, Nainital, Chopta and Tungnath, Kausani

May

Weather
Warm; cool in the high hills.
Snow
Snow at high altitude.
Roads
Open; heavy traffic on Char Dham routes.
Trekking
High season for many treks.
Char Dham
All four open; peak crowds.
Wildlife
Corbett is hot but active.
Festivals
Pilgrim season.
Crowds
Very high at hill stations and Char Dham.
Good picks
Mussoorie, Nainital, Kanatal, Dhanaulti, Kedarnath

June

Weather
Hot in the plains; monsoon arrives in the second half.
Snow
Snow remains only at high altitude.
Roads
Landslide risk increases at month end.
Trekking
Valley of Flowers opens; high-altitude treks.
Char Dham
Open; heavy crowds early.
Wildlife
Corbett: Dhikala and the other core zones close 15 June and Bijrani on 30 June; Jhirna, Dhela and Garjia stay open.
Festivals
Ganga Dussehra.
Crowds
Very high early; drops with monsoon.
Good picks
Chopta and Tungnath, Dhanaulti, Munsiyari, Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Sahib

July

Weather
Monsoon, with heavy rain.
Snow
None except at the highest altitudes.
Roads
Landslides and closures are frequent.
Trekking
Valley of Flowers in bloom; many other treks are unsafe.
Char Dham
Open but risky because of landslides and flooding.
Wildlife
Only Jhirna, Dhela and Garjia at Corbett stay open.
Festivals
Harela (Kumaon); the Kanwar Yatra brings heavy crowds and traffic restrictions to Haridwar and Rishikesh.
Crowds
Low at hill stations; high at Haridwar during Kanwar.
Good picks
Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Sahib

August

Weather
Monsoon continues.
Snow
None.
Roads
Landslides and washouts are common.
Trekking
Valley of Flowers; avoid most others.
Char Dham
Open but risky.
Wildlife
Only Jhirna, Dhela and Garjia at Corbett stay open.
Festivals
Raksha Bandhan, Janmashtami.
Crowds
Low.
Good picks
Valley of Flowers and Hemkund Sahib

September

Weather
Monsoon retreats; skies begin to clear.
Snow
Early snow at the highest altitudes.
Roads
Improving, though landslides are still possible.
Trekking
Many treks reopen late in the month.
Char Dham
Open; quieter than May-June.
Wildlife
Bijrani reopens 15 October; Dhikala and other core zones reopen 15 November.
Festivals
Nanda Devi fairs.
Crowds
Low to moderate.
Good picks
Chopta and Tungnath, Ranikhet, Kausani

October

Weather
Clear, dry and cool; excellent visibility.
Snow
Snow begins at high altitude.
Roads
Good.
Trekking
Prime season for most treks.
Char Dham
Open until closing in late October or November.
Wildlife
Bijrani reopens 15 October; Dhikala and other core zones reopen 15 November.
Festivals
Dussehra, Diwali (October or November).
Crowds
High around Diwali.
Good picks
Chopta and Tungnath, Kausani, Nainital, Mukteshwar

November

Weather
Cool to cold, clear.
Snow
First snow in higher areas.
Roads
Good; high roads may close later.
Trekking
Good until snowfall.
Char Dham
Closes; dates are announced each year.
Wildlife
Dhikala and other Corbett core zones reopen 15 November.
Festivals
Kartik Purnima ritual bathing at Haridwar.
Crowds
Moderate, then low.
Good picks
Jim Corbett National Park, Rishikesh, Binsar

December

Weather
Cold; clear days.
Snow
Snow in higher areas; Dhanaulti and Chopta may see snow.
Roads
Some high roads close.
Trekking
Winter treks begin.
Char Dham
Closed.
Wildlife
Good for birds and some zones.
Festivals
Christmas and New Year crowds in hill stations.
Crowds
High around Christmas and New Year.
Good picks
Auli, Chopta and Tungnath, Dhanaulti, Nainital
